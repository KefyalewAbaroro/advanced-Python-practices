Seasonal Consistency Across Years

Fall & Summer outperform Spring & Winter consistently across 2011–2013

🍂 Fall is the strongest season at $8.43M in sales — 28.7% of total revenue — driven primarily by Bikes.
🌸 Spring is consistently softest at $6.06M, roughly 28% behind Fall, likely reflecting a post-winter ramp-up period.
📊 Margins stay stable across seasons (47.3%–48.6%) — the seasonal swing is a volume story, not a pricing or mix story.
🔁 The pattern repeats every year — Fall & Summer beat Spring & Winter in each full calendar year (2011–2013), suggesting a durable, plannable cycle.
